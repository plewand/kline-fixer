# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work toward resolution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
